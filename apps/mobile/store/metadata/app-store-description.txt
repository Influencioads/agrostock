A bulk agricultural trade runs on a chain of phone calls. One broker to find the produce, another to argue the price, a transporter you used last season, a crew to load the truck.

AgroTraders puts that chain in one app. Source it, negotiate it, ship it, track it — from a single account.

Browse the marketplace without an account. Sign in when you're ready to transact.

SOURCE
• Search and filter listings by category, origin country, market, city and grade.
• Each lot carries a photo gallery, verified and grade badges, origin market, minimum order quantity, specs and buyer reviews.
• A quantity stepper converts units, so you can order in tonnes from a seller quoting per kilo.
• Put lots from several suppliers into one RFQ basket, grouped by supplier. Send each group out as its own request for quote, or check out in one pass — you name the destination once, each seller gets their own order and reference, and no seller sees what you bought elsewhere. Prices and stock are re-read at checkout.

NEGOTIATE
• Live ascending auctions with a countdown, the current highest bid, a masked bid history and a proxy ceiling that bids for you. No forced minimum raise.
• Reverse auctions: post what you need — product, quantity, deadline — and let sellers underbid each other. Competitors see only the count and the best price. Award the winner and it becomes an order.
• Public boards for both, refreshed live. Sellers turn a listing into a lot and see real names in their own bid book.

CONTROL THE HANDOVER
• Orders move through clear stages, and each party holds only its own code. The seller shows a pickup code, you show a delivery code, and the carrier enters both to open and close the trip.
• Raise a dispute from the order if something is wrong. Both sides review each other once it lands.
• Account balance with transaction history, PDF or CSV statements, an invoice centre, earnings with a withdraw request, and plan usage meters.
• Goods are settled directly between buyer and seller. The app collects no card or bank details.

SHIP AND STAFF
• Post freight requests and compare carrier quotes, or hire a transporter from inside an order so dispatch and the code handshake keep working.
• Shipment detail shows carrier, vehicle, driver and handshake timestamps, with origin, destination, road distance and ETA.
• Hire loading crews and labourers by city, with published rates and a question set for the job.
• Engage accountants, packers, processors, customs, finance and legal partners. A budget you commit to a hired provider is held until the work is confirmed complete.

RUN YOUR SIDE OF THE TRADE
One account can hold several roles, and the whole app changes when you switch consoles.
• Seller: publish listings, run offers and ads, open auctions, dispatch with a pickup code, take payouts.
• Transporter: quote on open loads, work trips through their stages, keep vehicles, drivers and routes.
• Loading and labour companies: publish what you supply and charge, assign teams, run daily attendance.
• Independent workers: accept shifts, check in and out, track earnings.
• Service providers: publish per-service prices and answer enquiries that arrive with the buyer's brief.

BUILT FOR CROSS-BORDER TRADE
• English and Russian throughout. Prices shown in 20 currencies at live rates, with the seller's quoted unit price preserved.
• Chat that translates incoming messages into your language, plus a community feed, groups and a support desk.
• Sign in with a password or a six-digit code emailed to you. Push notifications open the order, bid or shipment they refer to.
• Optional business verification and verified badges. Contact details stay masked, so both sides talk inside the app.

WHO IT IS FOR
Buyers, importers and wholesalers sourcing in bulk. Growers, traders and exporters selling. Freight carriers, loading companies, labour contractors and independent workers. Accountants, packers, processors and service partners.
