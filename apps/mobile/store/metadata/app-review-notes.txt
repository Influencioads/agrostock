2. PURPOSE
A B2B marketplace for agriculture: sellers of produce and equipment, buyers, transport operators, loading and labour contractors, service providers. It replaces a trade run on phone calls and brokers, where farmers cannot reach buyers beyond their contacts. Users publish what they have or need, search, negotiate and arrange transport; each counterparty carries a rating and trading history. Audience: adults in agriculture and logistics. Free, open to the public, no business registration required.

3. KEY PATHS
Browse: Home and Browse tabs (no account)
Order: listing > Buy Now > Checkout > Place order
Report/Block: "..." menu on any listing, auction lot, community post, profile, requirement or review; long-press any chat message
Plan & billing: Account/More > Plan & billing — auto-renewable In-App Purchase subscriptions; Restore Purchases on the same screen. Buyer and worker plans are not offered on iOS.
Terms/Privacy: Account > Legal (visible signed out)
Delete account: Account > Delete account

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
iOS binary: Firebase (Core + Cloud Messaging) only — Google receives the push token and notification text. No Analytics/Crashlytics/Performance; all other native modules are open-source RN/Expo libraries.
Server-side: our API + PostgreSQL (first-party JWT auth); Google Cloud Translation (listing, post and message text, cached; no identifiers, no training use); YooKassa/T-Bank/Robokassa (cards, website subscriptions only, on the acquirer's page); Google Places/Geocoding (~3,500-row Russian market list; city-to-coordinates); public FX feed (open.er-api.com); self-hosted object storage; SMTP email.
No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution, third-party sign-in, payment or mapping SDK; no web view; no OTA updates; no location or camera access — photo library and notifications only.

5. REGIONS
Consistent everywhere: no geo-gating, country list, IP geolocation or region-specific content. English and Russian, following device language, changeable anywhere. Display currency is a presentation preference only. Most sellers are currently in Russia, so the catalogue is weighted that way.

6. PAYMENTS AND IAP
Plans are sold through In-App Purchase, one subscription group per role. They only raise quotas: on the demo account, Account > Plan & billing lists the Seller plans (no role switch needed); buying Seller Standard raises the active-listing limit from 5 to 50. Every core function — browsing, buying, selling, messaging, reporting, blocking, deletion — is identical on the free plan. The app takes no other payment and has no card or bank field. All trade is physical goods or real-world services (3.1.3(e)); parties settle directly. Balances are topped up only on our website, via licensed acquirers.

7. UGC AND REGULATED GOODS
UGC is trade content only: listings with product photos, buyer requirements ("100 tonnes of rice wanted"), reviews and B2B messages. No general media sharing, video or social posting. Report and block are on every user-authored surface; reports reach a staffed queue within 24 hours. Blocking hides that person's content and stops contact. Deletion re-authenticates with the password, is immediate and permanent, and revokes all sessions and push tokens. Verification is optional; documents are stored privately and reviewed by staff, with no identity-verification vendor. Agrochemicals are listed by users, not by us; every new listing and every edit to its name, description, images, category or attributes is reviewed by a person before publication. Our Terms require sellers to hold every applicable licence.
